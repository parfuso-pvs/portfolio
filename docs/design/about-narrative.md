# PORT-701 - About narrative foundation

## Objective

Replace the About placeholder with a recruiter-oriented profile narrative that explains Phil's
working philosophy and range without turning the page into a second resume.

## Source boundary

- The August 25, 2026 resume supports the `8+ years` experience statement and current full-stack
  positioning.
- MEMX, Domani, PixelVerse Studios, and EarthCam names, roles, summaries, and hierarchy continue to
  come from the typed project registry.
- The narrative incorporates Phil's approved Q&A: systems thinking, full-stack ownership, early
  product involvement, independent product work, and after-hours experimentation.
- Unsupported rollout, component-library, traffic, and agency-performance percentages are excluded.
- Personal phone number, street-level location, and address details are not published.

## Narrative sequence

1. **Profile opening** establishes Phil as an engineer and curious builder, with the supported
   experience range as a restrained side note rather than a hero metric.
2. **The through line** connects platform work, independent products, and client work through one
   responsibility: making complex systems clear and trustworthy.
3. **How I work** defines systems thinking, full-stack building, product partnership, and continuous
   learning in a ruled dark-surface sequence.
4. **One evolving practice** uses four registry-backed contexts to show breadth without duplicating
   case-study narratives or inventing a chronological timeline.

## Composition

- The opening uses wide asymmetric typography and a blueprint layer rather than a portrait by
  default. A portrait remains unnecessary unless a later ticket establishes a specific purpose and
  approved asset.
- A raised thesis sheet and vellum evidence note continue the Human Assembly material language.
- The working principles use one continuous dark surface and ruled rows rather than equal cards.
- The context ledger returns to warm paper and collapses to one source-order column on mobile.

## Accessibility and state coverage

- The page contains one main landmark, one H1, labelled sections, ordered lists, and semantic asides.
- Decorative blueprint, grid, registration, and accent elements are hidden from assistive
  technology.
- The page is fully server rendered. Loading, empty, error, success, hover, focus, active, and
  disabled states do not apply to this static narrative ticket.

## Deferred

actions, and the final About-to-Contact close remain later tickets.
