# PORT-307 - Homepage contact close

## Objective

Complete the homepage with a decisive recruiting-focused invitation that gives visitors a clear
next action after reading Phil's featured work and supporting experience.

## Composition

- A full-width graphite sheet creates a deliberate visual stop after the warm paper sections.
- A faint technical grid, cobalt top tab, and ruled metadata preserve the Human Assembly system
  without introducing another paper card.
- The asymmetric 12-column composition aligns with the homepage while collapsing to one column on
  small screens.
- The primary action routes to Contact; the quieter secondary action routes to About.

## Content boundaries

- The role families mirror the approved project positioning in `AGENTS.md`.
- The section does not claim current availability, publish private contact details, or invent social
  profiles.
- The Contact destination remains the canonical place for future contact methods and form behavior.

## Interaction and accessibility

- The section has a labelled heading and remains fully server rendered.
- Both links meet the 44px minimum target and provide hover, keyboard-focus, and active states.
- The decorative grid and registration tab are hidden from assistive technology.
- Motion is limited to transform/color transitions and inherits the global reduced-motion override.

## Deferred

Contact methods, form handling, spam prevention, availability language, and resume delivery belong
to later route-specific tickets.
