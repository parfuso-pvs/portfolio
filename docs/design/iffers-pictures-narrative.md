# PORT-601 - Iffer's Pictures narrative chapters

## Objective

Establish the complete creative-ownership story beneath the shared Iffer's Pictures case-study frame
before approved photography and public-site evidence are introduced.

## Narrative sequence

1. **A personal brief** explains the Christmas-gift origin and direct collaboration with Phil's
   sister-in-law.
2. **Designing the frame** describes the approved photography-led, voice-led, and responsive design
   intentions without presenting unapproved imagery.
3. **Complete ownership** connects discovery, copy, art direction, interface design, and development
   while mapping the approved scope from the typed project registry.
4. **What the work demonstrates** positions the project as evidence of one continuous design and
   engineering process rather than a metrics story.

## Visual system

- The opening uses an offset raised sheet and vellum attribution note to distinguish this creative
  project from the dark technical openings used by MEMX and Domani.
- Ruled process rows create gallery-like pacing without generic cards or fake photo placeholders.
- The ownership chapter moves to the dark media surface so the full scope reads as the central proof.
- The final chapter returns to wide light-space composition before later approved photography.
- Desktop asymmetry collapses to source-order single-column layouts on small screens.

## Accuracy and attribution

- Phil independently owned discovery, copy, visual design, implementation, and direct collaboration.
- The site was a Christmas gift for Phil's sister-in-law.
- Iffer's Pictures is the exception to the normal PixelVerse partnership: Sami did not design it.
- No traffic, conversion, search, revenue, or performance claim is introduced.
- No generated or unapproved family, maternity, couple, portrait, or event image is used.

## State coverage

The narrative is static server-rendered content. Loading, empty, error, success, hover, focus,
disabled, and active states do not apply within this ticket's new surface.

## Deferred

Approved real photography, public-site presentation, any defensible project evidence, closing
navigation, and Iffer's Pictures published status remain separate follow-up tickets.
