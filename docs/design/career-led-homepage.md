# PORT-1001 - Career-led homepage experiment

## Objective

Validate a simpler recruiter-focused homepage that introduces Phil plainly and moves immediately
into his career progression. Preserve the Human Assembly palette and typography while removing the
layered hero, decorative diagrams, scroll-linked homepage motion, and project-gallery pacing from
the primary experience.

## Source boundary

- Phil approved the direction and supplied first-person positioning on August 29, 2026.
- The supplied resume confirms EarthCam employment from March 2018 through December 2019 and the
  July 2019 promotion from Junior Front-End Developer to Lead Front-End Developer.
- Phil explicitly confirmed that his MEMX employment ended in August 2026. This newer statement
  supersedes the resume's older `Present` label.
- The resume does not include MarketView dates. MarketView therefore appears only as an undated
  origin note using Phil's supplied title, Online Project Supervisor.
- Project names, links, ownership, attribution, and case-study claims continue to come from the
  typed project registry.
- Unsupported resume percentages, component counts, private address details, and phone details are
  not published.

## Experience model

- MarketView is a concise prologue explaining where Phil first used CSS and JavaScript.
- EarthCam and MEMX are the primary full-time company milestones.
- Each company contains visible role chapters so promotions read as progression within one job.
- PixelVerse Studios and Domani form a separately labeled parallel track for work completed outside
  full-time employment.
- Iffer's Pictures remains related evidence under PixelVerse rather than a separate job.

## Interaction direction

- The chronology is static and server rendered. Reading it does not depend on scroll position or
  animation.
- Role details remain visible instead of being hidden in an accordion, drawer, or modal.
- Existing case-study routes remain the detail destinations for MEMX, Domani, and Iffer's Pictures.
- EarthCam and PixelVerse link to durable anchors in the existing About chronology.
- The primary navigation is reduced to Experience, Work, About, and Contact. Individual projects
  remain discoverable through the career map and Work Index.

## Visual direction

- Preserve warm paper, graphite, cobalt, the editorial serif, and restrained technical labels.
- Use alignment, rules, type hierarchy, and one cobalt chronology line instead of stacked sheets.
- Keep the independent-work branch distinct through a cobalt rule and quiet paper surface, not a
  second animation system or competing accent.
- Mobile preserves the same company-role-project hierarchy in one reading column at 320px.

## Vertical-slice boundary

This experiment changes the homepage and simplifies global navigation. It does not delete the
previous homepage components, remove the selected Motion dependency, redesign case-study pages,
publish a resume, deploy the site, or merge the paused PORT-906 page-transition PR.
