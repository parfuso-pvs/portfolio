# PORT-1004 - Simplified MEMX career chapter

## Objective

Bring the flagship MEMX detail route into the restrained Living Career Thread system without losing
the technical evidence, attribution, or original non-proprietary diagrams that make the case study
useful to recruiters and engineering reviewers.

## Composition

- The route uses a dedicated career-led introduction with verified employment dates and two role
  chapters sourced from the shared experience registry.
- The MEMX name remains the shared transition participant and every return link targets the MEMX
  chapter on the homepage.
- The operating context and eight-market scale remain visible, but use quiet raised-paper surfaces
  instead of a dark dashboard-like panel.
- Technical progression remains a short three-part sequence after the employment chronology.
- The one strong cobalt surface is reserved for the central scaling decision.
- Production practice, team context, and the closing navigation return to warm paper.

## Diagram treatment

Both original system diagrams and their text equivalents remain. Their connector paths still explain
real configuration and delivery relationships, but blueprint grids, dark backgrounds, and unrelated
decorative rules are removed. The route-local Motion traces and complete reduced-motion result are
unchanged.

## Accuracy and scope

- Dates come from `src/content/experience.ts`.
- Project ownership, attribution, features, and shared metadata remain centralized.
- MEMX narrative and diagram content retain the confidentiality boundaries established by PORT-402
  and PORT-403.
- Domani and Iffer's Pictures keep their existing case-study frames until their own simplification
  tickets.
- No résumé, contact, deployment, analytics, or homepage changes belong to this ticket.
