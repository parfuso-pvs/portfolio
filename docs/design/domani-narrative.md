# PORT-501 - Domani narrative chapters

## Objective

Establish the product story beneath Domani's shared case-study frame before adding production media,
metrics, or store evidence.

## Narrative sequence

1. The origin explains the productivity-app tradeoffs that prompted Phil to build his own tool and
   frames Domani as a deliberate mobile-development experiment.
2. The planning loop explains evening planning, one top priority, categories, intentional rollover,
   and progress analytics as a coherent product behavior.
3. Independent ownership connects product strategy, design, implementation, release work, operation,
   and learning.
4. The product boundary renders shipped features and roadmap exploration directly from the typed
   project registry so future changes cannot silently blur the distinction.

## Visual system

- A dark evening-planning panel establishes the product's reflective starting point without imitating
  a phone screenshot.
- Ruled editorial rows present the planning principles as a repeatable sequence rather than feature
  cards.
- A blueprint surface frames independent ownership as an ongoing product-and-engineering practice.
- Shipped and exploratory capabilities use contrasting rule treatments and collapse into a single
  mobile reading order.

## Accuracy boundaries

- Phil is identified as Domani's creator, designer, engineer, and operator.
- Intentional rollover and progress analytics are presented as shipped capabilities.
- Nested checklists, bullet lists, and planning templates remain visibly labelled as exploration.
- No shared analytics identifier counts, inferred downloads, conversion rates, undated metrics, or
  unsupported commercial claims appear in this ticket.

## Interaction states

The narrative is static server-rendered content. Loading, empty, error, success, hover, focus,
disabled, and active states do not apply within this ticket's new surface.

## Deferred

Production screenshots or faithful product compositions, dated metrics, store evidence, and Domani's
case-study completion state remain separate follow-up tickets.
